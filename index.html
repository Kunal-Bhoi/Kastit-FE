    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Do I need Rhino or any CAD skills?

    No. You describe the ring in your browser. You get back a .3dm file, which opens in Rhino if your team uses it, and an .stl mesh that works with any 3D printer or slicer.

    What happens if my design isn’t supported?

    Kanvas tells you exactly which part it can’t build yet, such as the stone shape or the band style. It never quietly swaps in a similar-looking ring.

    How accurate is the metal weight?

    It is measured from the finished 3D model using the density of the alloy you chose, not looked up from a rough table.

    Can I describe a ring in my own words?

    Yes. Write it the way you’d brief a CAD designer: style, stone shape and size, metal and ring size. You can also share a reference image.

    Which jewellery can Kanvas make?

    Rings, today: 8 styles, 7 stone cuts, 2 band profiles and 8 metals. Pendants and earrings aren’t supported yet.
